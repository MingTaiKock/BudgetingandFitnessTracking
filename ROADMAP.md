# Little Wins — roadmap

Updated 10 October 2026 following the experience review, personal setup and gym planning implementation. See APP_EXPERIENCE_AUDIT.md for evidence and FEATURE_PLAN.md for acceptance criteria.

## Implemented first increment

- Persistent active workouts, routine snapshots, actual sets, per-exercise pending inputs, resume and explicit discard.
- Optional rest timer and elapsed-based editable duration; partial completion with duplicate protection.
- Money/workout actions at the top of Today and an unfinished-session prompt.
- Mobile workout layout with full-width fields and compact approved hamster art.
- Monthly-budget copy preview; unplanned months are labeled; new personal journeys have no assumed category limits.
- More readable secondary text and larger routine controls.
- Guided personal setup with names/currency, optional budget, starter routine selection, explicit review and save-before-replace behavior.
- A 24-exercise library across 10 equipment types, exercise replacement/reordering, optional planned load, and a recurring weekly schedule connected to Today. See GYM_PLANNING_DELIVERY.md.
- Previous matching exercise entries in guided workouts, exercise history with 30/90-day or all-time windows, and session recaps accessible from the diary. See EXERCISE_PROGRESS_DELIVERY.md.

Live browser/real-phone verification remains open because the preview connection timed out. This is implemented locally, not deployed.

## Next: gym planning and progress

1. Verify the implemented personal setup and daily flows on a real phone with the intended user.
2. Register and animate the approved Mochi/Chonk action art; keep illustrations distinct from exercise-form instruction.

## Then: daily convenience and reliability

Repeat money entries, editable savings goals, recurring-entry proposals with review, contextual progress labels and action-specific destructive copy. Installable/offline PWA, versioned migrations, dated backups and tested update behavior follow a real-user walkthrough.

## Later: optional connections

Only after demonstrated need: secure accounts/sync, explicit private partner sharing with revocation, then separately evaluated bank/wearable imports with duplicate detection and correction. No automatic sharing or inferred coaching prescription.

Mochi remains a small gray hamster; Chonk remains a larger brown hamster. The former cat/pixel direction is superseded. New art sheets are animation resources, not yet shipped exercise demonstrations.
