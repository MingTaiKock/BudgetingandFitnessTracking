## Mochi and Chonk artwork — persistent user requirement

Before generating, editing, or replacing hamster artwork, read `design/HAMSTER-STYLE.md` and visually inspect `public/prototype/habitat-clean.png`. The Habitat pair is the canonical character design for the entire app, including workout and reward artwork.

Keep real hamster proportions: compact, round, short and fluffy; tiny natural limbs and paws. Never elongate bodies or limbs to fit exercise equipment. Preserve Mochi's cream coloring and Chonk's golden coloring, Habitat facial proportions, soft fur and shading. Do not substitute a new mascot style.

Use actual reference images in generation, not just a textual description. Check new art beside Habitat and the approved counterpart before integration. User-approved pose corrections take precedence over older generated variants. Do not claim the existing workout collection is fully consistent without checking it; the style guide records remaining differences.

Preserve each original workout image's emotion and expression during rework. Habitat defines character identity, proportions, fur and rendering, not a single mandatory mood. Keep intentional closed happy eyes, open smiles, excitement, calm or exertion. Do not normalize every face to the calm curl reference. Attach the original pose alongside Habitat so its expression is preserved.
