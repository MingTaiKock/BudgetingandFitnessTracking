# Personal setup update — 10 October 2026

## Delivered

A four-step first-use flow with the approved Mochi and Chonk artwork:

1. Confirm names and currency, including the existing SGD default.
2. Choose category limits for this month or leave the budget unplanned.
3. Choose a weekly workout target and editable home/gym starter plans, both, or decide later. Exercise/rep previews are available before choosing.
4. Review all choices and explicitly create a personal journey. The demo replacement is explained and a download of the current demo is available first.

Back retains entered choices during setup. Exploring the demo does not apply those choices. Returning demo users can open setup directly from Start my journey; existing personal journeys bypass it. Successful completion opens Today with the daily actions already implemented in the previous update.

The builder rejects attempts to replace a personal journey through onboarding. It creates no sample transactions, workouts, savings, check-ins or rewards in the new journey. Saving to browser storage must succeed before the app replaces the visible demo or exits setup; a failure retains the review and its input values.
