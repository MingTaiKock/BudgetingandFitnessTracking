# Little Wins

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users and purpose

A personal, mobile-friendly budgeting and fitness companion for the user's girlfriend. Track money in SGD, follow preplanned exercise routines with rep ranges, and see improvement over time. This is a supportive gift with two recognizable characters.

## Brand commitments

Mochi is the small gray hamster. Chonk is the larger brown hamster coach. Both are hamsters; the early cat and pixel directions are superseded. Preserve the approved round proportions, faces, blush and gentle personality. References and generated art are documented in ART_REFERENCES.md and CUTE_POSE_RESEARCH.md. The user explicitly rejected generic substitute character art.

## Existing capabilities and constraints

React/TypeScript mobile web app. Manual budgets, money entries, savings records, workout diary, editable routines, check-ins and garden rewards. Records live in this browser with JSON export/restore. No account, bank connection, wearable sync, automatic partner sharing or deployed service. A manual savings record does not move money. Budget remaining is not bank balance.

## Product principles

- Make the next useful action easy to find.
- Preserve effort through interruptions and explain saving failures.
- Celebrate actual activity, including partial sessions and rest.
- Let the characters help without crowding inputs or prescribing performance.
- Keep sample data visibly separate from a personal journey.

## Open decisions

Workout schedule, available equipment, individual exercise preferences, deployment destination and future sharing permissions remain unconfirmed. Do not infer a medical fitness prescription, income, personal budget, or permission to share records.
